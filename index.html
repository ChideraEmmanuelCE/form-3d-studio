FORM.

A SMALL EXPERIMENT

Curiosity,
with a little depth.

FORM is a digital sculpture playground by Chidera Emmanuel. Three mathematical forms become objects you can turn, colour, and see in your own way.

There is nothing to sign up for and no right way to play. Your images are created on your device.

Take it for a spin

Touch or mouse — drag the sculpture to rotate. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or use the + and − controls.

Keyboard — focus the sculpture, then use the arrow keys to rotate, + / − to zoom, and 0 to reset. Press Escape to leave immersive view.

Make it yours — try a new shape, change the finish, or reveal the wireframe. Save a PNG or share a link to your shape and finish.
